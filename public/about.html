PHILRADS Staff Portal

The work tool of PHILRADS relief staff and volunteers

The PHILRADS Staff Portal helps Philippine Relief and Development Services, Inc. (PHILRADS), a Philippine relief and development organisation, coordinate disaster response: tracking relief goods, planning the team's work, seeing where people and places are, and keeping a record of what was done in the field. It is for PHILRADS staff and volunteers only; accounts are created by PHILRADS.

Staff sign-in Privacy Policy

What staff use it for

Relief stock

See relief goods in each warehouse and field hub, with every receipt, release and transfer recorded.

Tasks

Plan the day's work in the field and at the office, give tasks to teammates and track what's done.

Live Map

Warehouses, response sites and evacuation centres on one map, with weather, and team check-ins.

Documentation

Upload photos, videos and reports from the field as a record of relief work and as evidence for reports to partners.

Resources

Forms, policies, manuals and reports staff can open or download.

Why the portal asks for Google Drive access

PHILRADS keeps its field documentation in its own Google Drive. An administrator connects the PHILRADS Google account once, so that photos, videos and reports staff upload in the portal are saved straight into the PHILRADS Documentation folder in Drive (filed by who uploaded them and what they are), and so signed-in staff can view them in the portal. The portal does not use the access for anything else: it does not read, change or delete other files, files are never made public, and nothing is sold or used for advertising. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
