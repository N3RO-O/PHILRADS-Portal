PHILRADS Staff Portal

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The PHILRADS Staff Portal (philrads-portal.vercel.app) is an internal tool of Philippine Relief and Development Services, Inc. (PHILRADS) for its staff and volunteers. It is not open to the public: only people PHILRADS invites can sign in.

What the portal keeps

  • Your account: name, email address, role, and when you signed in (with the type of device and browser).
  • Your work: tasks, check-ins, places on the Live Map, records of relief stock, and the files you upload to Documentation.
  • Your location, only if you choose: when you check in or switch on “Share my live location” for a time you pick. Live sharing stops by itself; only your latest position is kept, never a trail.
  • An activity log of changes made in the portal, so PHILRADS can see who did what.

This information is stored with our service providers Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (hosting). Photos, videos and documents uploaded to Documentation are stored in PHILRADS’s Google Drive.

Google Drive

An administrator connects one PHILRADS Google account to the portal, once. The portal uses that access only to save files that staff upload to Documentation into the PHILRADS Documentation folder (in folders named after the uploader and the kind of file), and to show those same files back to signed-in PHILRADS staff. It does not read, change or delete any other files in that Google account.

The portal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside PHILRADS except the service providers above, as needed to run the portal. Files are never made public.

The connection’s access key is stored encrypted on our server and never sent to anyone’s browser. An administrator can disconnect it at any time, and the account owner can remove the portal’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who can see what

  • Signed-in PHILRADS staff see the work shared with the team, such as tasks given to them, the Live Map and Documentation.
  • Administrators can see the full activity log, including sign-ins.
  • We never sell personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Keeping and removing information

Records are kept while they are needed for PHILRADS’s relief work and reporting. To ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it removed, email info.philrads@gmail.com. Staff can also correct their own uploads and tasks in the portal.
