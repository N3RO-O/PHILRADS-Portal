Moving to prism.philrads.org

The browser treats a new address as a new app, so some things on each phone start fresh. Announce the move three days ahead and do it on a quiet day, not during a response.

What each person has to redo

What to do, in this order

  1. Add the domain in Vercel

    Project → Settings → Domains → add prism.philrads.org. Vercel shows the DNS record it expects.

  2. Add the DNS record where philrads.org is managed

    Type CNAME, name prism, value as Vercel shows it (usually cname.vercel-dns.com). Wait until Vercel says "Valid configuration". The HTTPS certificate is issued automatically.

  3. Allow the new address in Supabase

    Authentication → URL Configuration. Site URL https://prism.philrads.org. Redirect URLs: add https://prism.philrads.org/** and keep the old one until the grace period ends.

  4. Allow it in Google Cloud

    OAuth client: add https://prism.philrads.org to Authorized JavaScript origins, plus the Drive redirect URI on the new host. API key: add https://prism.philrads.org/* to the allowed websites.

  5. Point the IMS at the new address

    In the live IMS settings, change the sync target to https://prism.philrads.org/api/ims-sync and update any "Open PRISM" links.

  6. Claude updates the code and deploys

    New address in the Privacy Policy and push settings, the "PRISM has moved" banner for the old address, and (later) the Android link file.

  7. Test on two real phones

    Sign in, password reset email, Quick Unlock, a Drive upload, a push notification, an IMS sync, the Live Map with sharing on, and saving a task offline then syncing.

  8. Grace period: two weeks

    The old address keeps working with the banner, so offline work can sync. Then Vercel redirects philrads-portal.vercel.app to prism.philrads.org, and you remove the old address from Supabase and Google.

  9. Update links elsewhere

    philrads.org website, the Google consent screen, email signatures, printed QR codes, and the store listing.

PRISM Terms of Use
Effective [date of launch] · Version 1.0

1. About these terms

PRISM (Response, Inventory and Staff Management) is the internal work system of Philippine Relief and Development Services, Inc. ("PHILRADS", "we"), available at prism.philrads.org and as a mobile app. These terms apply to everyone who signs in to PRISM. By signing in, you agree to them and to the PRISM Privacy Policy. If you work for PHILRADS, these terms add to your employment or volunteer agreement and PHILRADS policies; where they conflict, your agreement and those policies apply.

2. Who may use PRISM

PRISM is not open to the public. Only staff, volunteers and partners invited by a PHILRADS administrator may use it, and only for PHILRADS work. You must be at least 18 years old, or [16 with written consent of a parent or guardian, if PHILRADS accepts younger volunteers].

3. Your account

  • Your account is personal. Do not share your password, your Quick Unlock device or a signed-in phone with anyone.
  • Keep your name, role and contact details correct.
  • Tell your supervisor or [support@philrads.org] right away if your phone is lost or stolen, or if you think someone else used your account.
  • You are responsible for what is done with your account until you report it.
  • Your administrator sets your role, which decides what you can see and change. Do not try to reach information your role does not allow.

4. Using PRISM properly

Use PRISM only for PHILRADS work, and enter information that is true and complete to the best of your knowledge. You must not:

  • record false stock counts, distributions, attendance, locations or reports;
  • copy, export, photograph or share PRISM information outside PHILRADS without permission from your supervisor;
  • upload anything unlawful, hateful, sexually explicit, or that infringes someone else's rights;
  • use PRISM to harass, track or monitor a person outside work duties;
  • try to break, overload, reverse-engineer or get around PRISM's security, or use automated tools against it;
  • use another person's account, or let someone use yours.

5. Information about the people we serve

PRISM holds personal information about beneficiaries, including names, households, locations and sometimes ages or needs. This information is confidential. Collect only what the task needs, use it only for the response or program it was collected for, and never share it on social media, personal chats or with anyone outside PHILRADS and its authorized partners. Follow the PHILRADS data protection and safeguarding policies at all times.

6. Location features

Live location sharing on the Live Map is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time. While it is on, your position is visible to the PRISM users your role allows, and check-ins you make are kept as part of the work record. PHILRADS uses location to coordinate teams and keep staff safe in the field, not to monitor you outside work. [If PHILRADS requires sharing during deployments, say so here and in the deployment policy.]

7. Photos, videos and documents

  • Ask for consent before photographing or recording a person. For a child, ask a parent or guardian.
  • Respect people's dignity: avoid images that show someone in distress, undressed or identifiable as a recipient of aid without their agreement.
  • Files you upload for PHILRADS work belong to PHILRADS and are stored in the PHILRADS Google Drive. PHILRADS may use them for reports, donors and communications in line with its policies and the consent given.
  • Upload only files you took yourself or have the right to share.

8. Connected services

PRISM works with other services to run: Supabase (accounts and data), Vercel (hosting), Google Drive (file storage), the PHILRADS Inventory Management System (stock records), and the push services of Google, Apple and Mozilla (notifications). Their own terms also apply to how they handle the data they keep. PRISM is not responsible for interruptions caused by these services.

9. Notifications

PRISM can send notifications on your device about tasks, mentions and inventory events. You can turn them off in your device settings or in PRISM. Notifications may show short work details on your lock screen, so set your phone accordingly.

10. Working offline

Some changes can be saved on your device without signal and sent when you are back online. Until they sync, they exist only on that device and can be lost if the app is removed or the device is reset. Sync as soon as you can, especially before leaving a deployment.

11. Ownership

PRISM, its design, name and logo, and the records in it belong to PHILRADS. You may not copy or reuse PRISM or its contents except for your PHILRADS work. Records you create in PRISM are PHILRADS work records.

12. Availability and changes

We work to keep PRISM available and your data safe, but PRISM is provided "as is" and may sometimes be unavailable, slow or incomplete, especially in areas with weak signal or during disasters. Do not rely on PRISM alone for urgent safety decisions; follow PHILRADS emergency procedures and use phone or radio when needed. We may change, add or remove features at any time.

13. Suspending or ending access

PHILRADS may suspend or end your access at any time, including when your work with PHILRADS ends or if you break these terms. Your access usually ends on your last day. Records you created stay with PHILRADS as required by law and its retention policy. You may ask for your account to be deleted from Profile → Account or by writing to [support@philrads.org]; see the Privacy Policy for what is kept and why.

14. Responsibility

To the extent the law allows, PHILRADS is not liable for indirect or incidental loss arising from your use of PRISM, or for loss caused by your device, your connection, or your breach of these terms. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under Philippine law that cannot be waived.

15. Privacy

How PRISM collects, uses and protects personal information is explained in the PRISM Privacy Policy at prism.philrads.org/privacy.html, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

16. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When we make an important change, PRISM will show you the new version and ask you to agree before you continue. The version and effective date are shown at the top.

17. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. Disputes will be brought before the proper courts of [Quezon City].

Privacy Policy · what to add

The current policy (public/privacy.html, 26 October 2026) already covers your account, work data, optional location, the activity log, Supabase and Vercel, and Google Drive. These additions bring it in line with RA 10173 and the store forms. Each block is ready to paste.

New · Data Protection Officer
PHILRADS has a Data Protection Officer who answers questions and requests about personal information in PRISM. Write to [dpo@philrads.org] or to the address below, marked "Attention: Data Protection Officer".
New · Why we may use your information
We process staff and volunteer information to manage your work with PHILRADS and to meet our legal duties. We process beneficiary information to deliver relief and development programs and to report on them, as our legitimate interest and, where the law requires, with consent. Location sharing and photos of people are based on consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting past processing.
New · People we serve, and children
PRISM holds information about people who receive help from PHILRADS, entered by our staff. Only staff whose role needs it can see it. We photograph people only with their consent, and children only with a parent's or guardian's consent. Beneficiaries can ask about their information through the Data Protection Officer.
New · How long we keep it
Account details: while you work with PHILRADS and [2] years after. Live location: only the latest position while sharing is on; check-ins are kept with the work record. Tasks, inventory and response records: [5] years, for audits and donor reporting. Activity log: [1] year. Photos and documents: as set by the PHILRADS records policy. After that, we delete or anonymize the information.
New · Where it's stored
PRISM data is stored with Supabase in [the region of your Supabase project, for example Singapore], served by Vercel, and files are kept in the PHILRADS Google Drive. These providers may process data outside the Philippines; we use providers with strong security and contractual protections, and we remain responsible for your information.
New · Notifications and the Inventory system
If you allow notifications, your device's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla) delivers short messages to you; we store a device address so we can reach it, and delete it when you turn notifications off or sign out. Stock records are copied into PRISM from the PHILRADS Inventory Management System so the Live Map and reports stay current.
New · Your rights
Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed, to access your information, to object, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, and to damages. You can ask for your account to be deleted from Profile → Account. Some records may be kept where the law or an audit requires it, and we will tell you which. If you believe your rights were violated, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
New · If something goes wrong
If a security incident puts your information at risk, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within 72 hours of learning of it, as the law requires, and tell you what we are doing about it.
Change · Address and version
Replace philrads-portal.vercel.app with prism.philrads.org, link to the Terms of Use, and show "Version 2.0 · Effective [launch date]". When this policy changes in an important way, PRISM will show you the new version when you next sign in.
