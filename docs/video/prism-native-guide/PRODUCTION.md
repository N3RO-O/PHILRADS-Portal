# PRISM field guide · actual app edition

The mobile screens are captured from the repository’s PRISM 1.9.7 build at a 412 × 840 viewport. The capture uses the app’s demo mode, fictional fixtures and local sign-in responses. The app’s welcome and sign-in imagery was anonymized for the video.

The edit keeps the original five-minute chapter structure and 15 vertical sharing cuts. The tap markers, type, eased reframing and animated prism light sit on separate visual layers around the captured interface. The narration and captions use the current **Can’t take it** label and the first-tap music behavior.

The capture scripts operate the actual PRISM interface against the local Vite demo server. `native-plan.json` maps the captures and captions to beats. `render.py` assembles the portrait, widescreen and sharing exports. The sign-in and Google Drive examples use demonstration responses and a sample document.
