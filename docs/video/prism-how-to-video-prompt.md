# PRISM how-to video: prompt

Paste everything below the line into your animation or video tool (or hand it to the animator). It describes PRISM as of version 1.9.4. Update the scenes for any modules that change before production.

---

## The brief

Make an animated explainer video that teaches PHILRADS staff how to use **PRISM by PHILRADS**, our Response, Inventory and Staff Management app. The audience is field staff and volunteers working on a phone, often outdoors, in a hurry, sometimes with weak signal. After watching, they should be able to sign in, find their tasks, record what they did and send proof from the field without help.

- **Length:** about 4 to 5 minutes for the full video. Also cut each scene as its own 20 to 40 second clip for sharing in group chats.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16 (phone-first) as the main version, with a 16:9 version for briefings.
- **Language:** clear, friendly English that is easy to translate into Filipino (Taglish subtitles welcome). Short sentences. Use the words exactly as they appear on screen.
- **Voice:** a calm, warm narrator, like a teammate showing you on their phone. Not a salesperson.
- **Show, don't tell:** every step is a finger tapping a real-looking phone screen, with the button it taps highlighted.

## Look and feel

- **Brand colours:** PHILRADS maroon `#8C1414` (headers, selected items), amber `#EC952A` (main buttons), cream `#FBF2F2` (text on maroon), dark slate surfaces `#1F2329` (dark mode), and white in light mode.
- **Logo:** a white prism outline. One beam goes in and comes out as three rays (cream, amber and red), meaning one system, three jobs. The wordmark is **PRISM** with the **P in red** and "RISM" in cream, and "BY PHILRADS" underneath.
- **Fonts:** Archivo Black for the wordmark and titles, Montserrat for everything else.
- **Motion:** soft and confident. Ease-out curves, gentle fades and slides, nothing bouncy or cartoonish. Mirror the app: the frosted-glass maroon header with drifting light, the P lighting up first and "RISM" fanning out after it.
- **Field setting:** cut between the phone UI and simple illustrated field scenes, such as a relief distribution, a warehouse, a flooded street or a training room. The staff are Filipino humanitarian workers in PHILRADS shirts.
- **Music:** open on the PRISM theme (the sign-in screen music), then keep a light, hopeful bed under the narration.

## Scenes

### 1. Opening (10 s)
Prism logo animation: a beam enters the prism and splits into three rays labelled **Response**, **Inventory** and **Staff**. The wordmark builds as the P glows red, then RISM appears. Narrator: "This is PRISM, PHILRADS's app for response, inventory and our team. Here's how to use it in the field."

### 2. Installing and signing in (35 s)
- Open the PRISM link in the phone browser and choose **Add to Home Screen**. The PRISM icon appears among the phone's apps.
- **Sign-in screen:** maroon glass header over a slow video of PHILRADS relief work, with the PRISM theme playing softly. A speaker button in the corner mutes it.
- **Staff:** type email and password, then tap **Sign in**. Show the eye button that reveals the password, and the card shaking once on a wrong password.
- **Forgot your password?** sends a reset link by email.
- **Volunteers:** tap the **Volunteer** tile ("Email code"), enter email, tap **Email me a code** and type the 6-digit code from the email. No password needed. Only invited emails work.
- **Guests:** the **Guest** tile ("Look around") shows what's coming for visitors.
- **Quick unlock:** after the first sign-in, set a 6-digit PIN or fingerprint or face so reopening the app is quick.
- **Safety note:** staff accounts sign out after 5 minutes without activity (a "Still there?" countdown shows first). With a PIN set up, it locks instead. Sharing your live location keeps you signed in.

### 3. The welcome and Home (30 s)
- A short welcome greets you by name with the time of day. Tap **Let's go**.
- **Home** is your dashboard:
  - the welcome band with **View my tasks**
  - the Verse of the Day
  - **My tasks**, with three columns: *Needs your answer*, *Today* and *Coming up*
  - the calendar with deadlines and Philippine holidays
- **Top bar:**
  - **Search** finds pages, people, tasks, projects, statistics and resources.
  - The faces of who's online open the **Active** list.
  - The **bell** holds notifications.
  - Your **picture** opens the account menu.
- Show the menu opening on a phone. The order is Home, Live Map, Staff Tasks, Inventory Management, Response, PDM, Documentation, Projects, Forms, Resources, Statistics, then Staff & volunteers and Logs for admins.

### 4. Notifications (20 s)
A note pops in with a chime. Tapping it (or the phone notification when the app is closed) opens the right task or page. In the bell, switch between **All** and **Unread**. The account menu has switches for the notification sound and the error sound. Your status can be Online, Idle, Do not disturb or Invisible; Do not disturb keeps it quiet.

### 5. Staff Tasks: the heart of field work (60 s)
- **Phone views:** three tabs, **Mine**, **Given** and **Team**. Lists follow the six PHILRADS programs: Emergency response, Rehabilitation and recovery, Sustainable livelihood, Training and capacity building, Special projects, and Monitoring and coordination.
- **Task requests:** a task someone asks you to take on waits at the top of **Today**. Tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Doing the work:** tap **Start** when you begin. When you finish, tap **Finish** and add a short note, links and photos. Swipe a task right to finish it, or left to plan it for **Today**.
- **Needs a photo:** some tasks can only be finished with a photo, so show taking a photo of a distribution. Any other task earns +5 XP for a photo.
- **Steps:** tick off a task's steps as you go.
- **Who controls a task:** only the person who gave it can change it, reassign it, send it back or remove it. If they send it back, you get a note saying what still needs doing.
- **Giving a task:** the **New task** button floats at the bottom right. Choose the person or the whole team, a due date, a list and a priority.
- **Motivation:** your week shows as seven circles, filled on each day you finish a task (+25 XP for a full working week). You also earn levels, badges by program and a team goal.

### 6. Live Map (35 s)
- The map shows warehouses, response sites, project and activity places, and team members (their picture in a ring with their first name).
- **Share live location** while in the field, with a floating bubble that has **Stop**. Or **Check in once**.
- Tap a place to see live stock and people served.
- Extras: weather and temperature, full screen, and **Show all places**.
- Narrator tip: "Share your location while you're out, so the team knows you're safe and where help is."

### 7. Inventory Management (30 s)
Relief goods across warehouses and field hubs. Show these steps:
- searching and filtering stock
- an item marked **running low**
- the history of **receipts**, **releases** and **transfers**
- exporting the list

Note: stock records come from the PHILRADS IMS, so PRISM shows the same numbers the warehouse sees.

### 8. Response (25 s)
Logging and coordinating a disaster response, from rapid needs assessment to distribution and reporting. Create a **relief event**; once approved, the IMS confirms it and it appears on the Live Map.

### 9. Documentation (30 s)
Upload photos, videos, PDFs and reports from the field straight to the right Google Drive folder, with each file's record kept in PRISM. Show the floating **Upload** button, choosing the project or activity, and the **All / Media / Records** filters. Narrator: "Upload while you're there. Don't wait until you're back at the office."

### 10. Forms (25 s)
PHILRADS forms filled in on screen, laid out like the paper originals. Show:
- typing into a form
- making text bigger or smaller
- **signing on screen** with a finger
- printing or saving as PDF, each on the right paper size

### 11. Projects, Resources and Statistics (30 s)
- **Projects:** each program and project with its milestones, deadlines, team, tasks, documentation and total cost. Give one task to several people at once.
- **Resources:** forms, policies, manuals, reports and brand files from Google Drive. Use **Preview**, or the ⋯ menu for Open in Google, Download and Copy link. Category chips slide in one row.
- **Statistics:** every PHILRADS project and activity since 2023, showing where, when and who was served, also on the Live Map. Funding and donors are visible only to administrators and coordinators.

### 12. PDM (5 s)
PDM is coming soon. Show its menu item with a "Coming soon" label.

### 13. Your profile and settings (20 s)
- **Profile:** tap your photo to change it (drag and zoom), add your position and a short bio, and choose an avatar frame (some are earned with levels). A switch makes the profile public or private.
- **Account menu:** Dark, Light or Auto theme, the sound switches, What's new and **Sign out**.

### 14. For coordinators and admins (20 s, optional cut)
- **Staff & volunteers:** invite people, choose each person's role (volunteers see the Live Map, Tasks, Forms, PDM and Documentation), and switch accounts on or off.
- **Logs:** everything that happens in PRISM, with flags to look into. Nobody can change or delete it.

### 15. Field tips and closing (20 s)
Quick tips as captions:
- Sign in with a PIN or fingerprint so it's fast in the field.
- Share your live location while you're out.
- Finish tasks with a photo as proof.
- Upload documentation on the spot.
- Weak signal? PRISM keeps working on what's already loaded, and the app tells you when you're offline.
- Updates arrive automatically; when "What's new" appears, tap to update.

Close on the prism logo and the line: **"One system, three jobs: response, inventory and our team. PRISM by PHILRADS."**

## Do and don't

- Do use real button names exactly as written above (bold), so staff can match them on screen.
- Do show a phone held in one hand, with buttons in thumb reach.
- Don't show real names, emails, phone numbers or locations of staff or beneficiaries. Use made-up names and blurred faces.
- Don't show funding amounts or donor names.
- Don't invent features that aren't listed here.
